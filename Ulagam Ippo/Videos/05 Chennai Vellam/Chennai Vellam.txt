Chennai floods explained in Tamil: why Chennai floods every year during the north-east monsoon. Chennai is a flat, low-lying coastal city; the Cooum, Adyar and Kosasthalaiyar rivers drain slowly and high tides and storm surge block their outflow. The old chain-linked eris (lakes / tanks) that stored rain water were filled in – T. Nagar was built on the drained Long Tank and Valluvar Kottam stands on the old Nungambakkam lake. The Pallikaranai marsh, Chennai's natural sponge, has lost about 90% of its area to buildings, roads and a garbage dump. Clogged storm water drains, plastic waste, and very heavy rain in a few days from cyclones (Tambaram got about 49 cm on 1 December 2015). The same city ran dry in the 2019 Chennai water crisis. Solutions: restore lakes, rainwater harvesting, no plastic in drains.
