Kumari Kandam explained in Tamil, part 3: plate tectonics. Earth's plates move a few centimetres a year; India and Madagascar were joined until about 90 million years ago, which explains the lemur fossils without any sunken continent. The deep Indian Ocean south of Kanyakumari, thousands of metres down, shows no evidence of one.
